This study evaluates the energy performance of a residential 1-to-4 DC-inverter variable refrigerant flow (VRF) system. Cooling EER was measured and calculated under a range of indoor temperature setting modes and indoor-unit ON/OFF combinations, and the main influencing factors were analysed in Excel.

The results provide practical recommendations for energy-efficient system design and for user behaviour in residential VRF applications.
