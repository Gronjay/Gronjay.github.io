My research sits at the intersection of **building physics, HVAC engineering and control**. I am interested in how models learned from operational data can be used to operate buildings more efficiently without compromising comfort — the core idea behind model predictive control (MPC) for building energy systems.

## Model predictive control for building energy systems

*Current — MSc research, HKUST · with Prof. Wang Zhe*

- Building a foundation in MPC formulation for building thermal dynamics: state-space models, prediction horizons, comfort constraints and cost functions.
- Interested in reducing the modelling burden of MPC by combining physics-based reduced-order models with data-driven identification from building automation system (BAS) data.
- Target applications: VRF/VRF multi-split systems and central HVAC plants in subtropical climates such as Hong Kong.

## Energy efficiency of residential multi-split air conditioners

*May 2026 · submitted to Journal of Appliance Science & Technology*

- Measured and calculated the cooling EER of a 1-to-4 DC-inverter VRF system under different temperature setting modes and indoor-unit ON/OFF combinations.
- Analysed the influencing factors in Excel and translated the results into practical recommendations for energy-efficient design and user behaviour in residential VRF systems.

## Differentiated thermal comfort control scheme

*Apr 2025 · Provincial-level College Student Innovation and Entrepreneurship Program*

- Proposed time-based transition strategies and spatial zoning for open-plan spaces.
- Introduced visual recognition and trajectory prediction for non-core occupants while protecting privacy through anonymisation.
- Applied differentiated control strategies for core vs. non-core personnel and integrated phased power regulation and renewable energy to reduce building energy consumption.
- Combined multiple sensors and AI algorithms into an intelligent HVAC regulation system.

## Energy efficiency inspiration from traditional architecture

*Jul 2024*

- Conducted on-site experiments in ancient ancestral halls, measuring indoor temperature, humidity and air velocity.
- Analysed the thermal environment with Excel and MATLAB and reviewed the literature on human thermal comfort.
- Compared the energy-saving potential of traditional architectural structures with modern building energy consumption.

## Field and industry work

Alongside modelling, I have spent time in the field: energy audits of commercial buildings, VRF field tests in Turpan, material-chamber experiments for a near-zero-emission building, HVAC unit manufacturing, and foundation/weld inspection. This applied experience shapes the questions I bring to modelling work.
