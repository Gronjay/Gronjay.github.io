I am a Master of Science student in **Environmental Engineering and Management** at the [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)](https://hkust.edu.hk), where I work with Prof. Wang Zhe on **building energy systems** and **model predictive control (MPC)**. My goal is to make buildings comfortable and low-carbon by combining physical models, operational data and control algorithms.

I completed my B.Eng. in **Building Environment and Energy Engineering** at Guangzhou University in 2026, graduating with an average score of 86/100 (major ranking 8/91) and the **National Scholarship**. My undergraduate work ranged from VRF air-conditioning efficiency and differentiated thermal-comfort control to passive cooling lessons drawn from traditional architecture.

Through internships at the **China Academy of Building Research**, an HVAC manufacturing line, and construction and inspection companies, I have audited real buildings, processed millions of sensor records and tested equipment in the field. Those experiences keep my research grounded in how buildings actually perform.

## Research interests

- Model predictive control for building energy systems
- HVAC and VRF multi-split air-conditioning efficiency
- Data-driven building energy audits and LLM-assisted diagnostics
- Thermal comfort, zoned control and occupant behaviour
